BookTrack's fonts, served from this folder so the app makes no third-party
requests and works offline.

  bricolage-grotesque.woff2  Bricolage Grotesque (headings)     weights 600–700
  dm-sans.woff2              DM Sans (text)                     weights 400–600
  fraunces.woff2             Fraunces (titles on designed covers) weight 600
  source-serif-4-roman.woff2 Source Serif 4 (member editorial headings) weights 200–900

The first three are from Google Fonts (latin subset) and licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org

Source Serif 4 is the unmodified variable Roman WOFF2 from Adobe's release
repository: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif
Original: WOFF2/VAR/SourceSerif4Variable-Roman.ttf.woff2
Its SIL Open Font License is included in source-serif-LICENSE.md.
